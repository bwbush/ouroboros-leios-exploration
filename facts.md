## Upstream Artifacts

- **Transaction identity precision varies by trace path in the deployed w40 node** (verified 2026-10-08; layer: implementation, `8206f9f843d46f9fa9a58449ed2387e2c0aa1a84`): mempool attempt/admission/rejection/removal transaction objects carry an unconditional eight-character ID; manual removals mix that formatter with a renderer that truncates only at minimal detail. Block-fetch transaction-ID arrays are full at non-minimal detail and omitted at minimal detail. Current aggregate extracts do not join on shortened transaction IDs, but transaction-lifecycle analyses must not treat prefixes as unique keys. [Source-backed audit and analytical implications](arc-leios-ha/baselining/logging.md#october-8-abbreviated-transaction-ids-and-analytical-joins).

- **The Musashi operational Terraform roots now share versioned S3 state with native locking** (verified 2026-10-08; layer: deployed infrastructure tooling, Terraform 1.15.0): six existing roots covering 122 managed resource instances and the new six-resource backend root were migrated without changing their resource records or outputs. Seven refreshed plans showed no changes, and overlapping plans demonstrated lock exclusion followed by successful reuse. The separate bucket is encrypted, private, and tagged; old local states are preserved outside the working roots. This verifies Brian's access and same-key locking, not Will's effective permissions, which remain an acceptance item. [Migration evidence and recovery metadata](arc-leios-ha/baselining/terraform/state-backend/migration-2026-10-08.md).

- **The pinned w40 transaction tracing cannot uniformly suppress bodies while preserving full transaction identifiers using detail settings alone** (verified 2026-10-08; layer: node implementation, `8206f9f843d46f9fa9a58449ed2387e2c0aa1a84`): the [Shelley-family transaction formatter](https://github.com/IntersectMBO/cardano-node/blob/8206f9f843d46f9fa9a58449ed2387e2c0aa1a84/cardano-node/src/Cardano/Node/Tracing/Era/Shelley.hs#L94) always emits only eight hexadecimal characters in `txid`, adding the transaction representation exactly at `DDetailed`. [Mempool rejection and removal events](https://github.com/IntersectMBO/cardano-node/blob/8206f9f843d46f9fa9a58449ed2387e2c0aa1a84/cardano-node/src/Cardano/Node/Tracing/Tracers/Consensus.hs#L1087) also omit error detail below `DDetailed`. [Peer transaction replies](https://github.com/IntersectMBO/cardano-node/blob/8206f9f843d46f9fa9a58449ed2387e2c0aa1a84/cardano-node/src/Cardano/Node/Tracing/Tracers/NodeToNode.hs#L209) print the transaction list regardless of detail level. Compact full-identifier event tracing therefore needs event-specific handling or a formatter change, not a global detail reduction. Current Asian-relay archives from October 8 contain full transaction structures and witnesses in these paths; they are textual representations, not a guaranteed replayable binary archive.

- **Lifecycle-audit implementation facts** (verified by source reading during the 2026-09-17 accuracy review of the [transaction-lifecycle diagram](arc-leios-ha/background/pre-scoping/leios-node-tx-lifecycle.svg); layer: implementation @ ouroboros-consensus `b56977b`):
  - The **minimum certification gap is a minimum wait** (`currentSlot − announcementSlot ≤ minGap ⇒ Nothing` in `decideLeiosCertify`), and certification must occur in the announcing RB's direct successor — so a successor RB elected *inside* the gap strands the pending EB. Enforcement is **forge-side only**: follower validation carries `FIXME: Check the min certification gap` (`Forker.hs:618`), with no other validation-side use of `getMinCertificationGap`.
  - A **CertRB's wire body carries the Leios certificate and no regular transactions** (`mkBody`: tx sequence `Seq.empty`, certificate via `leiosCertBlockBodyL`); "empty body" comments are shorthand.
  - **Missing-closure CertRB parking is implemented**: `ignoreUnacquiredCertRB`/`...Suc` transiently hide a CertRB and its successors from chain-selection candidates until the closure arrives; `leiosAcquiredEbsRunner` + `chainSelReprocessLeiosEb` reprocess on `AcquiredEbTxs`.
  - **Fetch priority is two-tiered** (`fetchPriorityTiers`): EBs within L = 3·L_hdr + L_vote + L_diff of the current slot are requested oldest-first, staler EBs then freshest-first; slot unknown (syncing) ⇒ one oldest-first tier.
  - **Vote timing is wall-clock-stubbed**: window opens at announced-slot onset + `lHdrWait = 3 s`, deadline + `lVoteWindow = 4 s` later (both TODO-marked to become ledger protocol parameters); the check runs immediately if the closure completes after the window opens.
  - **Fetching is incremental, but voting validation is whole-closure gated rather than streamed**: fetched transaction jobs are inserted as they arrive, while `runLeiosVoting` schedules an EB only after LeiosDb emits `AcquiredEbTxs`; `validateEbClosure` then resolves and classifies the complete closure before serial application. The source explicitly lists validation during streaming as a TODO (verified 2026-09-23; layer: implementation @ `b56977b`).
  - The ≤ 2-announcements-per-election limit is enforced per upstream peer by `PeerState`/`ElState` (third or repeat ⇒ disconnect); `ElBimap` is the downstream relay gate for equivocation evidence.
  - **Offer gating is two-level**: LeiosDb emits `AcquiredEb` on body insert (→ `MsgLeiosBlockOffer`) and `AcquiredEbTxs` only on closure completion (→ `MsgLeiosBlockTxsOffer`); a completed SQLite row stores `missingTxCount = −1` as the notified sentinel.
  - Mempool `Sync` is bounded by a **converging off-lock catch-up loop** (`syncDeltaCap = 256` under-lock target, `syncMaxIters = 8` safety valve); `reapplyUntilTimeout` bounds *snapshot* computation, not `Sync`. Default mempool capacity is 2× the ordinary block measure, byte-overridable; the CIP's ≥ 2 × (RB+EB) formula awaits `ouroboros-consensus#2280`.
  - **Certified closure adoption skips transaction validation but remains serial**: [`resolveAndApplyLeiosClosure`](https://github.com/IntersectMBO/ouroboros-consensus/blob/b56977baae0740f563060a8a9171c78be865b357/ouroboros-consensus/src/ouroboros-consensus/Ouroboros/Consensus/Storage/LedgerDB/Forker.hs#L948-L985) batches the closure's UTxO ledger-table key read, then [`applyLeiosClosure`](https://github.com/IntersectMBO/ouroboros-consensus/blob/b56977baae0740f563060a8a9171c78be865b357/ouroboros-consensus-cardano/src/shelley/Ouroboros/Consensus/Shelley/Ledger/Leios.hs#L158-L218) uses a sequential `foldM` over the Dijkstra `LEDGER` rule with `ValidateNone`, so transaction signatures and Plutus scripts are not rechecked. The certificate licenses that trust. The prototype does not implement the post-CIP constraint model's input-DAG antichain scheduler; voting's [`validateEbClosure`](https://github.com/IntersectMBO/ouroboros-consensus/blob/b56977baae0740f563060a8a9171c78be865b357/ouroboros-consensus/src/ouroboros-consensus/LeiosVoting.hs#L1981-L2160) is also a serial apply/reapply recursion (verified 2026-09-23; layer: implementation @ ouroboros-consensus `b56977b`).
  - Demo fetch-job caps: `maxJobBytesSize = 64 KiB`, `maxJobTxCount = 20000`. LeiosDb EB statuses are not one linear chain: certified 0→1→2, cleanup 0→3 and 2→3, and promotion rescues 3→1.

- **Quorum mechanics** (layer: implementation @ pins above): seat weight = pool's share of total active stake (`Weight = Rational`, committee weights sum ≤ 1); vote acceptance tallies `totalW ≥ threshold` with re-votes replacing; `verifyLeiosCert` enforces bitfield well-formedness, rejects any signer bit on a keyless seat, requires `Σ seatWeight ≥ τ`, and verifies one aggregate BLS12-381 min-sig signature — matching CIP-0164's `Σ stake(votes) ≥ τ·stake_total-active`. Committee = **top-N_c pools by stake, deterministic** (CIP-0164 "stake-based truncation"; weighted Fait Accompli with Local Sortition (wFA^LS) is explicitly the earlier-drafts scheme), seated by the ledger SNAP rule on the mark snapshot for epoch e+1; keys honored while `epoch < registeredIn + maxKeyAge`.
- **Certificate aggregation is aggressive and freezes the first threshold-crossing signer set** (verified 2026-09-23; layer: implementation @ consensus `b56977b`): [`LeiosVoteState.addVote`](https://github.com/IntersectMBO/ouroboros-consensus/blob/b56977baae0740f563060a8a9171c78be865b357/ouroboros-consensus/src/ouroboros-consensus/LeiosVoteState.hs#L79-L179) calls `aggregateLeiosCert` immediately when its running received-vote weight first reaches τ and memoizes the result; post-threshold votes update the tally but do not rebuild the certificate. A forger later obtains that cached value through [`queryCert`](https://github.com/IntersectMBO/ouroboros-consensus/blob/b56977baae0740f563060a8a9171c78be865b357/ouroboros-consensus-diffusion/src/ouroboros-consensus-diffusion/Ouroboros/Consensus/NodeKernel/Forge.hs#L288-L356). Therefore a pool absent from an on-chain certificate did not necessarily fail to vote; it may have arrived after quorum at that node.

- **Musashi's pool economics and rotation clocks** (read 2026-09-21 from the pinned `shelley-genesis.json`; layer: deployed network — governable, so confirm with `query protocol-parameters`): `poolDeposit` **500 ₳**, `keyDeposit` **2 ₳**, `minPoolCost` **170 ₳** (a floor on `--pool-cost`), `minUTxOValue` 1 ₳, fees *a* = 44 / *b* = 155,381. Epochs are 21,600 slots at 1 s = **6 hours**, so stake delegated in epoch *N* is active at the start of *N+2* — a wait of **6–12 hours** from an arbitrary point in epoch *N* — and Leios committee seating uses the same snapshot boundary. Two rotation clocks coincide at ≈93 days: Key Evolving Signature (KES) (`maxKESEvolutions` 62 × `slotsPerKESPeriod` 129,600 s) and the Boneh–Lynn–Shacham (BLS) key (`maxKeyAge` 374 epochs), but renewing the BLS key means **re-registering the pool**, not reissuing a certificate. `cardano-node` checks only the **verifiable random function (VRF)** signing key's file permissions at startup (`checkVRFFilePermissions`, rejecting any group or other permission), not KES.
- **Pool ΘΕΛΩ activation state** (verified 2026-09-22 from the submitted registration and faucet delegation; layer: deployed musashi network): pool `88610017a37cfcbf497127b1be43efd71376bd52f3bb8773b3dcf838` registered and received its faucet delegation in epoch 62; the producer pod is deployed, and the stake becomes active at the start of epoch **64**, 2026-09-23 00:00 UTC. Activation makes the pool eligible for Praos leadership and Leios committee participation; it does not guarantee a block-production slot.
- **A pool's Leios voting key is registered through its pool registration certificate, not a separate transaction** (verified 2026-09-21 by source reading at cardano-ledger `1587f21`; layer: implementation): `sppBlsKey :: StrictMaybe BlsKey` on [`StakePoolParams`](https://github.com/IntersectMBO/cardano-ledger/blob/1587f21a7d1306dc590c2749a5c66232ef66aad0/libs/cardano-ledger-core/src/Cardano/Ledger/State/StakePool.hs#L460-L472), where `BlsKey = { blsPubKey, blsPossessionProof }` over BLS12-381 min-sig. The ledger keeps `BlsKeyState { bksKey, bksRegisteredIn }`, the epoch stamp that the `maxKeyAge` (374-epoch) expiry is measured from. On the node side the operator supplies the signing key with `--shelley-bls-key` ([`Parsers.hs:420`](https://github.com/IntersectMBO/cardano-node/blob/7e33674108ed17eeeda3a25e98b66a610cbd99ff/cardano-node/src/Cardano/Node/Parsers.hs#L420), "Path to the BLS (Leios) signing key").

Giorgos Panagiotakos's topology result: an attacker who knows the **stake-sampled** connections (3 per node) and picks a bad initial diffusion set weakens EB-diffusion guarantees, with **11% of stake having zero outgoing stake-sampled edges at 50% adversarial stake** (2026-09-01). By contrast **T23 (withhold-then-burst) showed no measurable harm** up to 100 EBs / 3.6 h stale / 150 ms links — the pull-based EB fetch self-regulates (Christopher Tilt, 2026-09-06, preliminary).
